Service Name: Easypaisa PSID Payment

How to Pay via Easypaisa Using PSID:
Step 1: Log in to the official Easypaisa app.
Step 2: Open See All.
Step 3: Open Others.
Step 4: Select 1 Bill Invoice/Voucher.
Step 5: Enter the PSID exactly as shown on the official payment slip. For the Paymir flow covered here, the app expects a 17-digit PSID.
Step 6: Review the institution or biller name, reference details, amount, due date, and payment status.
Step 7: Confirm the payment only when the retrieved details match the official slip.
Step 8: Save the successful receipt and transaction reference.
Step 9: For a step-by-step visual guide, visit https://www.youtube.com/shorts/0SPCIBrM57g
