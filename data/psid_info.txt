=== Service Start ===
Service Name: What is PSID

Short Answer:
PSID stands for Payment Slip ID. It is a unique payment reference generated by the institution whose fee, tax, bill, or service charge you need to pay. The PSID connects your payment to the correct application, challan, bill, or government service.

Key Facts:
- The issuing institution generates the PSID; a bank or wallet does not create it for you.
- The PSID is normally shown on a payment slip, challan, application portal, SMS, or email.
- Entering the PSID in a supported payment channel retrieves the payment details associated with that reference.
- A PSID is a payment reference, not a bank-account number, card number, PIN, or OTP.
- Keep the payment receipt and transaction reference after completing a payment.

Official Reference:
1LINK 1BILL information: https://1link.net.pk/products-services/bill-payment-service

Service Name: How to Generate PSID

Short Answer:
You do not manually create a PSID inside Easypaisa, JazzCash, or a banking app. The relevant institution generates it after you submit an application, request a service, create a challan, or select an online-payment option.

How to Obtain a PSID:
Step 1: Open the official portal or application of the institution you need to pay.
Step 2: Complete the required application, registration, challan, or service-request form.
Step 3: Select the available online or digital payment option.
Step 4: Generate or view the payment slip or challan.
Step 5: Copy the PSID exactly as displayed and note the amount and due date.
Step 6: If the PSID is not displayed, check the application history, payment section, registered SMS messages, or email.

Important:
- Do not use a PSID copied from another person or an older application.
- If the institution does not generate a PSID, contact that institution rather than your bank or wallet.
- If a PSID expires, return to the issuing institution to renew it or generate a new payment slip, if that service permits it.

Service Name: How to Verify PSID

Short Answer:
Verify a PSID by checking it through the official portal that issued it or by using the bill-inquiry screen of a supported payment channel. The displayed institution, amount, reference, due date, and payment status must match your payment slip before you confirm.

How to Verify a PSID Before Payment:
Step 1: Copy the PSID directly from the official payment slip, challan, portal, SMS, or email.
Step 2: Open the issuing institution's official portal or a supported Easypaisa, JazzCash, or banking payment channel.
Step 3: Select the relevant 1BILL, Invoice/Voucher, bill-payment, or PSID-payment option.
Step 4: Enter the PSID exactly as issued.
Step 5: Review the institution or biller name, payable amount, applicant or reference details, due date, and payment status shown by the system.
Step 6: Continue only if the retrieved details match your official payment slip.

Stop and Seek Help If:
- No payment details are returned.
- The institution, amount, or reference does not match.
- The PSID appears expired, cancelled, or already paid unexpectedly.
- The payment channel asks you to send money to a personal account instead of processing the bill through its official payment screen.

Contact the issuing institution for PSID-data problems. Contact the bank or wallet for transaction-processing problems. Never share your PIN, OTP, password, or complete card details with anyone claiming to verify a PSID.

Service Name: PSID Format and Details

PSID Format:
For the Paymir and 1BILL payment procedures covered by this assistant, the payment screen expects the numeric PSID shown on the official slip, commonly a 17-digit value. Enter it without spaces or extra characters unless the issuing institution explicitly instructs otherwise.

Important Details:
- Always use the exact PSID printed or displayed by the issuing institution.
- Identifier lengths can differ in payment systems outside the scope of this assistant, so the official slip is the authoritative source.
- A new application, reassessment, or payment event may generate a different PSID.
- Do not guess missing digits or reuse a PSID from a previous transaction.
- Check the amount and institution returned by bill inquiry before paying.

Service Name: PSID Payment Security

Is PSID Payment Secure?
PSID payments handled through official 1LINK-enabled banking and wallet channels use established payment infrastructure. Security still depends on using the genuine institution portal or official app and checking the retrieved bill details before confirmation.

Safety Checklist:
- Use only the official website or app of the issuing institution, bank, Easypaisa, or JazzCash.
- Check the institution name, amount, reference, and due date before confirming.
- Never share your PIN, OTP, password, card CVV, or complete card number.
- Do not approve a payment when the displayed details differ from the official slip.
- Avoid payment links received from unknown people or unverified social-media accounts.
- Save the receipt and transaction reference after payment.
- If you suspect fraud, immediately contact the payment provider and issuing institution through their official support channels.

Official Reference:
1LINK security and payment infrastructure: https://1link.net.pk/about-us/

Service Name: PSID Payment Troubleshooting

Issue 1: Payment failed and no money was deducted
- Confirm that the PSID was entered correctly and has not expired.
- Check that the payment channel supports 1BILL Invoice/Voucher or PSID payment.
- Verify your account balance and internet connection, then try again once.
- If the error continues, use another supported channel or contact the bank or wallet.

Issue 2: Payment failed but money was deducted
- Do not immediately repeat the payment, because this can create a duplicate-payment risk.
- Check the app's transaction history and the issuing institution's payment status.
- Save the transaction reference, time, amount, and screenshot of the status.
- Reversal and reconciliation times vary by provider; contact the bank or wallet for its official timeframe.
- If the institution still shows unpaid, contact both the payment provider and issuing institution with the transaction reference.

Issue 3: PSID shows as already paid
- Check the issuing institution's portal and your payment history.
- Confirm whether someone authorized paid on your behalf.
- If the status is unexpected, contact the issuing institution with the PSID; do not attempt another payment until the status is clarified.

Issue 4: PSID expired or was cancelled
- Return to the issuing institution's official portal.
- Check whether the payment slip can be regenerated or renewed.
- Use only the newly issued PSID and discard the expired or cancelled reference.

Issue 5: PSID is not accepted or no bill is found
- Re-enter the PSID exactly as displayed, without spaces or extra characters.
- Confirm that you selected the correct 1BILL, Invoice/Voucher, or PSID menu.
- Check whether the PSID is active and supported by that payment channel.
- Contact the issuing institution if the reference still returns no details.

Issue 6: Payment details do not match the slip
- Do not confirm the payment.
- Recheck the PSID and compare the institution, reference, amount, and due date.
- Contact the issuing institution if the mismatch remains.

Issue 7: Payment succeeded but confirmation was not received
- Check the bank or wallet transaction history and save the transaction reference.
- Check the issuing institution's application or payment-status page.
- Allow the provider's stated processing time for status synchronization.
- Contact the issuing institution if the payment status is not updated, and contact the payment provider if the transaction itself is missing or disputed.

Support Information to Keep Ready:
- PSID
- Issuing institution
- Payment amount
- Transaction reference
- Date and approximate time
- Screenshot of the transaction status

Never include your PIN, OTP, password, card CVV, or complete card number in a support request.
