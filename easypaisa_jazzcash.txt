Service Name: Easypaisa PSID Payment

How to Pay via Easypaisa using PSID:

Step 1: Log in to the Easypaisa App

Step 2: Go to See All

Step 3: Go to Others

Step 4: Select 1 Bill Invoice/Voucher

Step 5: Enter your 17-digit PSID

Step 6: Make sure the amount shown on the slip is the same as the amount shown on the payment platform, such as JazzCash, Easypaisa, or a bank.

Step 7: For a step-by-step visual guide, visit: https://www.youtube.com/shorts/By7rhGDBJU8

Service Name: JazzCash PSID Payment

Purpose:
Step-by-step guide to paying using a PSID through the JazzCash mobile app.

How to Pay via JazzCash using PSID:

Step 1: Log in to the JazzCash App

Step 2: Go to Other Payments & Services

Step 3: Go to Corporate Payments

Step 4: Select 1 Bill (Invoice and Voucher)

Step 5: Enter your 17-digit PSID

Step 6: Make sure the amount shown on the slip is the same as the amount shown on the payment platform, such as JazzCash, Easypaisa, or a bank.

Step 7: For a step-by-step visual guide, visit: https://www.youtube.com/shorts/By7rhGDBJU8
